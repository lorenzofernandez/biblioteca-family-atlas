# Author–publication chronology audit

Reviewed 49,780 catalogue records and 21,532 author entries. Checked 22,141 dated links where author life dates were available.

- 179 links predate the candidate birth.
- 280 links imply an author younger than 12.
- 30 low-confidence primary matches with multiple pre-birth non-reference records were moved to alternatives.
- Posthumous dates alone did not trigger identity removal. Later editions, reprints and translations can explain them.
- No book dates, source transcriptions, catalogue records or author relationships were changed.
- Other flagged cases remain a review queue, not confirmed errors.
