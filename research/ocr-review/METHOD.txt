Catalogue OCR and data-quality review, 18 October 2026

All 49,780 page/card identifiers in the current catalogue were submitted to Apple Vision accurate text recognition. The earlier bulk pass used fast recognition at 1,200 pixels. This pass renders to a maximum 1,800-pixel width using PDFKit, recognizes Italian, French, English, German, Spanish and Portuguese with language correction disabled, and records text, alternative candidates, line boxes and recognition scores.

Cards with changed headings, changed publication-date extraction, possible text loss, large transcription differences or weaker recognition receive a second pass at 2,200 pixels with language correction enabled. A focused Cassini/Bergman comparison also uses 2,400 pixels. This is a second setting of the same OCR engine, not independent human confirmation. Recognition scores are not calibrated accuracy percentages.

The first accurate pass is the default transcription. Text with large disagreements or possible loss is held for review. Visual inspection supplies explicitly recorded corrections for selected cards. Latin-script lookalike glyphs are normalized in overwhelmingly Latin lines; raw OCR remains retained separately. Historical spelling is not systematically modernized. New dates require agreement of the two accurate settings and must occur in the publication/citation area, excluding the title, page counts and shelfmarks. Previously reviewed date corrections remain authoritative.

Titles are reconstructed from contiguous card lines, stopping at citations/imprints/physical descriptions and joining explicit line breaks. Reviewed editions are protected. Author consolidation requires consistent full source-card names and compatible authority metadata. Surname/initial-only speculation is not a merge rule. Cassini and Bergman have separate documented decisions. Original card text and the work's scholarly attribution remain distinct, including a Cassini card that itself names the wrong generation.

Every updated title is locally compared against the previously retrieved Internet Archive and Crossref reference corpus. Added leads require close title, creator surname and same-year agreement. This is not a new individual web search for every book. Matches remain tentative work-level leads; no surviving-copy estimate or verified physical-copy claim is inferred.

Stable card IDs, file/page references, researched editions, surviving-copy research, reviewed biographies and author redirects are preserved. The history file records before/after text and metadata. The review queue retains unresolved OCR disagreements and the chronology queue flags publication years preceding a proposed author's birth. Posthumous publications are not treated as errors.

Reproduction:
  Compile recognize.swift with swiftc -O, then run:
  recognize ORIGINAL_PDF_DIRECTORY INPUT_PAGE_MANIFEST OUTPUT_JSONL SHARD WORKERS LANGUAGE_CORRECTION MAX_WIDTH
  The program checkpoints by file and page and can safely resume.
  prepare.py stages transcription changes; authors.py proposes full-name consolidation;
  rematch.py screens cached bibliography metadata; apply.py integrates the reviewed stage.
  Intermediate OCR files and baseline snapshots are expected in ../ocr-review beside the checkout.
